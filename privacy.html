Static · North Point station · 1974

Privacy

Static collects nothing about you and sends nothing to us. The night stays on your phone.

Effective 10 November 2026 · Developer: Vadym Magerko

01What the app does with data

  • No server, no accounts. Static has no server of its own and no sign-up or sign-in. The name you give the night operator is part of the game and stays on your iPhone. The whole season, its words and fonts are inside the app.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking. No third-party SDKs, advertising identifiers, pixels or crash reporting. We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your progress stays on your device. The nights you finished, the words in your sky, the notebook and your settings are kept only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. We never see them. Settings → Start the season again erases them; deleting the app erases everything.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Only while you hold the microphone button to answer Mara, Static uses Apple's Speech framework to turn your English reply into text and see which reply you chose. On iPhones that support it, recognition happens on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio on its servers under Apple's privacy policy. Static does not record, store or send that audio. Every reply can also be sent by touch.
  • Voices. Mara and the other voices are made while you play by Apple's speech synthesis on your iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere to make them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. Payment, your Apple ID and payment details stay with Apple. The app only learns whether Season One is unlocked on your Apple ID.
  • Sharing. "Share" at the end of the season opens the system share sheet with a short text. You choose where it goes; the app does not know who received it.

02Children

Static is made for adult beginners. It has tense story moments (knocks at a door, a cry for help, a storm; Gentle mode softens them) and nothing else unsuitable for children. Because we collect no personal data at all, we collect none from children either. Purchases by family members may need a parent's approval through Apple's Ask to Buy.

03Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Your data on your device is under your control as described above. If you have a question, write to us.

04Changes

If Static ever starts collecting any data, we will update this page and the App Store privacy answers first, and change the effective date above.
