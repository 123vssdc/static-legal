?Questions

What is free?

Nights 1 to 3 of Season One, forever, with no account and no trial timer. Replays, the sky of words and the notebook stay yours.

What does Season One open?

Nights 4 to 14 and the ending: the storm, the notebook that writes back, and who Mara is. It is a single payment, not a subscription: nothing renews.

How do I restore a purchase on a new iPhone?

Settings → Restore purchases, or "Restore purchases" on the purchase screen. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

I tapped buy but the nights did not open

If Ask to Buy is on in your family, the request went to the family organizer. Nothing is charged until it is approved; the nights open as soon as it is.

I hear nothing

Turn the sound on with the speaker button and turn up the volume; headphones are best. The voices are made by your iPhone's own speech synthesis. For the best sound, download an Enhanced or Premium English (US) voice in iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. With sound off, the game still shows the words.

Static does not hear me

Check that Microphone and Speech Recognition are on for Static in iPhone Settings → Static. Hold the microphone button, say the reply, then let go. Every reply can also be sent by touch: "Send without voice".

The knocks and the storm are too much

Settings → Gentle mode makes them softer and stops the screen from shaking.

Can the hints be in my language?

Hints and menus are in English or Ukrainian (Settings → Hint language). The English on air always stays English.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
